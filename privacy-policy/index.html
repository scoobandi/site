Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Scoob & I Dog Training and Digital Dog School (collectively, “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respect your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our websites, complete a quiz or form, interact with our advertisements, purchase products or services, book an appointment, or otherwise communicate with us.

This policy applies to scoobandi.com, digitaldogschool.com, and forms, checkout pages, scheduling pages, communities, and other digital experiences we operate or link to from those websites.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, billing and transaction information, appointment information, messages, quiz and survey responses, and information about your dog, training goals, behavior history, or safety concerns that you choose to provide.

Information collected automatically

We and our service providers may collect your IP address, browser and device information, approximate location, pages viewed, links clicked, video interactions, referring source, dates and times of visits, cookies, advertising identifiers, and similar usage information.

Information from other sources

We may receive information from advertising platforms, social networks, analytics providers, payment processors, scheduling tools, and customer-management platforms when permitted by their policies and your settings.

2. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • provide dog-training services, courses, resources, quiz results, consultations, community access, and customer support;
  • process payments, fulfill purchases, schedule appointments, and maintain business records;
  • send requested information and service-related email or text messages;
  • send educational or promotional communications when permitted by law and your choices;
  • personalize recommendations and follow-up based on information you submit;
  • operate, secure, troubleshoot, analyze, and improve our websites, advertising, content, and services;
  • measure advertising performance and attribute visits, leads, bookings, and purchases; and
  • prevent fraud, enforce our terms, protect safety and rights, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Email and text messages

You may unsubscribe from marketing email using the link in an email. Transactional or service-related messages may continue when needed to provide something you requested or purchased.

If you affirmatively opt in to text messages, we may send automated texts about your quiz or inquiry, training resources, appointments, customer service, programs, and offers described at the point of consent. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help. Consent to marketing texts is not a condition of purchase. See our Text Messaging Terms.

Mobile information and consent: We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers, text-message originator opt-in data, or consent records with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. We may disclose information to service providers that help us deliver messages, but they may use it only to provide services to us. Text-message opt-in consent is not transferred to third parties.

4. Cookies, analytics, and advertising

We may use cookies, pixels, local storage, and similar technologies for site operation, analytics, attribution, security, and advertising. Providers may include Google Analytics, Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Microsoft Clarity, TikTok, and similar services. These providers process information under their own privacy terms. You can limit cookies through browser or device settings, although parts of our websites may not work as intended.

5. How we disclose information

We do not sell personal information for money. We may disclose information:

  • to vendors that provide hosting, customer relationship management, email and text delivery, scheduling, payment processing, analytics, advertising, course delivery, community, fraud prevention, and professional services;
  • when you ask us to or direct us to connect with another service;
  • to comply with law, legal process, or valid government requests;
  • to protect our rights, users, business, animals, or the public; or
  • in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of business assets.

Service providers may access information only as reasonably necessary to perform their work for us and are expected to safeguard it.

6. Payments and third-party services

Payment card information is generally collected and processed by payment providers such as Stripe or PayPal rather than stored directly by us. Our websites may link to third-party sites or services. Their practices are governed by their own policies, and we are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

7. Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain customer and transaction records, honor communication preferences, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal, tax, accounting, and safety obligations. Retention periods vary by the type and purpose of the information.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of certain personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request, and legal exceptions may apply.

To make a request, email joel@scoobandi.com. You may opt out of marketing email through its unsubscribe link and opt out of texts by replying STOP.

9. Children’s privacy

Our commercial services and lead-generation forms are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Anyone under 18 should use our services only with the involvement and permission of a parent or legal guardian. If you believe a child provided personal information without appropriate permission, contact us so we can address it.

10. International visitors

We operate from the United States. If you access our services from another country, your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other countries where our providers operate, which may have different data-protection laws.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date identifies the current version. Material changes may also be communicated through our services or by other appropriate means.
