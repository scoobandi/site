Before you enroll

Questions that matter.

What happens if I miss a live session?

Replays will be available. Live attendance and participation are part of the Do-The-Work Guarantee requirements.

How do the video reviews work?

Submit your training videos before each Q&A. Joel will review them and give you specific feedback during the live session.

How much preparation is required?

Less than one hour of prerequisite education is released as soon as you enroll. Complete it before the first live lesson on October 20.

How much practice should I expect?

Plan to work with your dog every day during the sprint. Twenty minutes three days a week will not be enough to keep pace. This is for owners ready to apply the work, not merely watch the lessons.

Is Digital Dog School really included?

Yes. You receive immediate access after purchase and keep Digital Dog School as a lifetime reference. It normally sells for $147.

What is the Do-The-Work Guarantee?

Complete the preparation, show up to the classes, practice wholeheartedly, submit videos, participate in the Q&As, and contact Joel along the way if you need help. If by the end of Fix the Walk you still have not made significant progress toward a more enjoyable walk with your dog, Joel will issue a full refund.

Is this appropriate for serious aggression or bite history?

Not as a first step. If your dog has seriously injured someone or cannot be safely handled outside, begin with a private consult.

Fix the Walk is $597. Any financing options shown at checkout are provided by a third party; Scoob & I is not offering an internal installment plan.
